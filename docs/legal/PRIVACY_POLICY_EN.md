## 10. Your Rights

Under GDPR and Algerian law, you have the right to:

| Right | Description | How to Exercise |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Access** | Obtain a copy of your data | Settings > Export |
| **Rectification** | Correct inaccurate data | Settings > Profile |
| **Erasure** | Delete your data | Settings > Delete account |

| **Portability** | Receive data in standard format | Settings > Export |
| **Restriction** | Restrict processing | privacy@iafactory.ai |

**Response time**: Maximum 30 days
