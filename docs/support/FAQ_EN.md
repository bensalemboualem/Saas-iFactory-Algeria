## 2. Account and Registration

### How do I create an account?

1. Go to [app.iafactory.ai](https://app.iafactory.ai)
2. Click "Sign up"
3. Enter your email or use Google/GitHub
4. Confirm your email
5. You're ready!

### Can I use IAFACTORY without an account?

No, an account is required to:
- Save your conversations
- Manage your credits
- Access all features

### How do I delete my account?

1. Go to **Settings** > **Account**
2. Click "Delete my account"
3. Confirm by email

⚠️ This action is irreversible. All your data will be deleted within 30 days.

### I forgot my password

1. Click "Forgot password" on the login page
2. Enter your email
3. Click the link received by email
4. Set a new password

## 4. Features

### How does AI chat work?

1. Select a model (GPT-4, Claude, etc.)
2. Type your message or question
3. The AI generates a response
4. Continue the conversation or start a new one

### Can I upload files?

Yes, supported formats are:
- **Documents**: PDF, Word, TXT, Markdown
- **Images**: PNG, JPG, WebP (for visual analysis)
- **Code**: All text files

**Limit**: 20 MB per file, 100 MB per conversation

### What is an AI agent?

An agent is an autonomous AI that can:
- Execute complex multi-step tasks
- Use tools (web search, calculation, etc.)
- Interact with external APIs

**Examples**: Research agent, coding agent, data analysis agent

### How do I create a workflow?

1. Go to **Workflows** > **New**
2. Drag and drop action blocks
3. Connect them to define the flow
4. Test and activate

## 6. Privacy and Security

### Are my conversations private?

Yes, absolutely:
- Encrypted in transit and at rest
- Not used to train models (unless consented)
- Not shared with third parties

### Is IAFACTORY GDPR compliant?

Yes, we comply with:
- GDPR (Europe)
- Law 18-07 (Algeria)
- Right to access, rectify, delete your data

### Where is my data stored?

- **Main servers**: Europe (France/Germany)
- **Backup**: Algeria

### How do I enable two-factor authentication?

1. **Settings** > **Security**
2. Click "Enable 2FA"
3. Scan the QR code with an app (Google Authenticator, Authy)
4. Enter the verification code

## 7. Technical Support

### The chat isn't working, what should I do?

1. **Check your internet** connection
2. **Refresh** the page (Ctrl+F5)
3. **Clear browser** cache
4. **Try** another browser
5. If the problem persists, contact support

### The AI gives incorrect answers

AI can sometimes:
- Give outdated information (knowledge cutoff)
- "Hallucinate" false information

**Tips**:
- Verify important facts
- Rephrase your question with more context
- Try another model

### How do I report a bug?

1. Email: support@iafactory.ai
2. In-app: **Help** > **Report a bug**
3. Discord: #bugs channel

**Include**: screenshot, steps to reproduce, browser/OS
